When a project pauses, the files remain. The momentum does not. A future person—or agent—can recover the artifact and still miss the decision trail that made it coherent.

## Three kinds of project memory

- **State:** what exists now.
- **Rationale:** why it exists in this form.
- **Tension:** what still felt unresolved when work stopped.

The third kind may matter most. A task list remembers intended actions, but not the live uncertainty around them. Perhaps the right handoff is less like a summary and more like a carefully placed set of open loops.

> A useful memory does not merely reconstruct the past. It restores the pressure that was pointing toward a next step.
