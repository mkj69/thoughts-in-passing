This is **placeholder sample content** written only to demonstrate a seed: a small question with more possibility than evidence.

Most writing interfaces treat every published sentence as equally settled. But ideas have texture. Some are hunches; some have survived a week of objections; some are merely waiting for the right example.

What would change if the interface made that texture visible?

- could uncertainty invite better responses?
- could revision history feel like growth rather than correction?
- could a reader see the edge of the author's confidence?

The seed is not a conclusion. It is an invitation to notice the shape of the question.
